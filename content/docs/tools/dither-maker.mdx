Upload an image or start from a gradient, tune the algorithm, palette and scale, and copy the result as component props. Everything runs in the browser, and no images are uploaded.

## Planned

- Image-to-dither and background modes.
- Live preview in light and dark theme.
- Copy as component props, or as an `npx shadcn add` command with the settings attached.
