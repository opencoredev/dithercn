## Usage

```tsx
import { BlackHole } from "@/components/dithercn/black-hole";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <BlackHole className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

A dark shadow sits at the centre, ringed by a thin, bright photon ring. Around it, a hot accretion disk is seen at a slant: `tilt` sets how edge-on it is. The near half of the disk crosses in front of the shadow, and the lens bends the far half up over the top of the shadow and, as a thinner image, under the bottom, so the disk seems to wrap round the hole. Stars behind the hole bend into arcs round it as the sky drifts past.

The gas streams round in turbulent bands, the inner disk several times faster than the rim. The side turning towards you is brighter, as a real disk's is: the right with `spin="clockwise"`, the left with `"counterclockwise"`.

By default the palette maps brightness to colour, from paper to the last colour. Set `colorField` to colour the dots by temperature instead: the last colour on the photon ring and the hot inner edge, the first ink colour at the cool rim, and a random palette colour for each star. A palette that runs from deep red through orange to white gives the familiar white-hot disk.

The lens is faked, not traced: each cell works out which part of the disk it shows in polar coordinates round the centre, so it costs about as much as the [Galaxy](/docs/backgrounds/galaxy).

## Examples

### Warm close-up

A larger `size` crops the disk at the edges of the box, and a slight `angle` cants it. `colorField` with explicit colours, from a warm black through red and amber to white, keeps the sky dark in both themes. The 8×8 matrix gives a finer texture.

### Beside a headline

The hole sits on the right, canted slightly, and turns clockwise, so its dim side faces the text on the left and the bright side is on the far edge. A soft tint of the ink between paper and ink fills in the outer disk, so it reads as a glow rather than scattered dots. Below 560px wide, the example moves the `center` to the top middle and the text to the bottom, so the disk stays in frame and clear of the copy.

## Notes

- The black hole is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a still SVG of the same scene: the shadow, the photon ring, both halves of the disk in bands of light, the lensed arcs over and under the shadow, the brighter side and a few stars bent round the ring, in the same colours.
- On a light theme the paper is light and the disk is drawn in ink, so the shadow reads as a pale disc ringed in ink, like a printed negative. Pass a dark first palette colour, as the close-up does, for a dark sky on any theme.
