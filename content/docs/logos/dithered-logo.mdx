Logo walls look mismatched because every brand uses different colours. Dithering them all into theme ink gives the row a single consistent look.

A few placeholder logos will ship with the first release. Logo submissions will open after that.

## Planned

- SVG and PNG input, rendered in theme ink.
- A `LogoWall` layout with even optical sizing.
- Hover to show the original logo colours.
