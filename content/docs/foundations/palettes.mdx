By default a dither is two-tone: ink and paper. Ink comes from `--dither-ink` and paper from `--dither-paper`, and both fall back to your shadcn `--primary` and `--background` tokens. Change your theme and every dither follows it, dark mode included.

## Planned

- Two-tone by default, read live from CSS variables, so a theme switch re-renders without a reload.
- Multi-step palettes built from `--chart-1` to `--chart-5` for charts and heatmaps.
- A `palette` prop for one-off overrides that still accepts CSS variables.
- Contrast checks in development, with a console warning when ink and paper are too close to read.
