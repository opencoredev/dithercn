dithercn is a [shadcn registry](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/registry) of dithered backgrounds, charts, interface pieces, type and images. Each piece is copied into your project as source, reads its colours from your shadcn theme, and works in light and dark mode.

## What a dither is

A dither draws a smooth tone using only a few colours. It places dots of ink at a density that matches the tone, so your eye blends them into a gradient. Dithering began as a workaround for limited screens and printers. It now reads as a deliberate texture, a little like risograph print, and it's what dithercn is for.

## How the docs are organised

- [Concepts](/docs/concepts/dither-engine) explain `dither-core`, the engine every component shares: threshold matrices, palettes, cell size, motion and performance.
- [Backgrounds](/docs/catalog#catalog-backgrounds) are full-bleed surfaces for heroes, sections and cards.
- [Charts](/docs/catalog#catalog-charts) are charts and status displays with a dithered fill.
- [Interface](/docs/catalog#catalog-interface) pieces are skeletons, loaders, dividers, cards, empty states and a 404 block.
- [Text](/docs/catalog#catalog-text) and [Images](/docs/catalog#catalog-images) dither your headings, pictures and logos.
- [Tools](/docs/catalog#catalog-tools) let you make and export dithers without writing code.

The [catalog](/docs/catalog) lists every component on one page.

## Principles

- **Your theme, not ours.** Dithers read your `--primary` and `--background` tokens through the [`--dither-*` variables](/docs/theming), so they change when your theme changes.
- **Quiet by default.** Motion is opt-in and slow, and every animated piece holds still when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- **Cheap on dashboards.** Charts are static SVG patterns, and animated backgrounds draw one cell per pixel and stop when they scroll out of view.
- **Real text stays text.** Dithered headings are still HTML, so they can be selected, read aloud and indexed.
